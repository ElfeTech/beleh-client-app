## 1. Definitions

- **"Applicable Data Protection Law"** means all laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data under this DPA, including the **UAE Personal Data Protection Law** (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 and its implementing regulations), the **EU General Data Protection Regulation** (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, **"GDPR"**), the **UK GDPR** and Data Protection Act 2018, and the **Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection**, in each case as applicable to the relevant processing.
- **"Controller"**, **"Processor"**, **"Data Subject"**, **"Personal Data"**, **"Personal Data Breach"**, **"Processing"**, and **"Supervisory Authority"** have the meanings given in the GDPR, or the equivalent meanings under other Applicable Data Protection Law.
- **"Customer Personal Data"** means Personal Data contained within Customer Data that Yulona processes on the Customer's behalf through the Service.
- **"SCCs"** means the Standard Contractual Clauses annexed to European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.
- **"UK Addendum"** means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the SCCs issued by the UK Information Commissioner.
- **"Subprocessor"** means a third party engaged by Yulona to process Customer Personal Data.
- Other capitalised terms have the meaning given in the **Terms of Use**.

## 2. Roles and scope

**2.1 Roles.** In respect of Customer Personal Data, the **Customer is the Controller** (or is a Processor acting on behalf of its own controller) and **Yulona is the Processor** (or subprocessor). Each Party will comply with its own obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law.

**2.2 Independent controller data.** Yulona acts as an **independent Controller** in respect of account data, billing data, support communications, and Service usage and telemetry data, as described in the **Privacy Policy**. This DPA does not apply to that processing.

**2.3 Precedence.** In the event of conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Use in relation to the processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails. Where the SCCs apply and conflict with this DPA, the SCCs prevail.

**2.4 No variation.** The Customer's own data processing terms, vendor forms, or security schedules do not apply and are expressly rejected unless agreed by Yulona in a signed writing.

## 5. Yulona's processing obligations

**5.1 Documented instructions.** Yulona will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which comprise the Terms of Use, this DPA, the Customer's configuration and use of the Service, and any further written instruction agreed by the Parties. Yulona may process where required by law to which it is subject, in which case it will inform the Customer of that requirement before processing unless the law prohibits it.

**5.2 Unlawful instructions.** If Yulona considers that an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law, it will inform the Customer without undue delay and may suspend performance of that instruction until it is withdrawn, amended, or confirmed.

**5.3 No training on Customer Personal Data.** Yulona will not use Customer Personal Data to train, fine-tune, or improve general-purpose AI models, and has contracted with its AI model Subprocessors on terms that prohibit their use of Customer Personal Data for model training. Yulona may generate and use **aggregated, de-identified** statistics about Service usage that do not identify the Customer, any Member, or any Data Subject and do not reveal the contents of Customer Data.

**5.4 Confidentiality.** Yulona will ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are bound by an appropriate obligation of confidentiality and are subject to appropriate access controls and training.

**5.5 Data minimisation by design.** Yulona will maintain the architecture described in Annex I, under which record-level data from Connected Sources is retrieved at query time rather than replicated persistently, subject to the persistence described in Annex I clause 6.

## 10. Personal Data Breach

**10.1** Yulona will notify the Customer **without undue delay**, and in any event within **48 hours**, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data in systems under Yulona's control.

**10.2** The notification will include, to the extent then known and as it becomes available: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point for further information. Yulona may provide information in phases as the investigation progresses.

**10.3** Yulona will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate, and remediate the breach, and will preserve relevant evidence.

**10.4** The Customer is responsible for determining whether the breach requires notification to a Supervisory Authority or to Data Subjects, and for making any such notification. The Customer will not name Yulona in any public statement or regulatory filing about the breach without Yulona's prior written consent, except where legally required.

**10.5** Yulona's notification is **not an acknowledgement of fault or liability**.

**10.6** Yulona is not responsible for a Personal Data Breach originating in the Customer's own systems, network, endpoints, identity provider, Connected Sources, or Workspace configuration, or arising from credentials supplied by the Customer that exceeded read-only, least-privilege scope.

## 14. Return and deletion

**14.1 During the term.** The Customer may delete Customer Personal Data at any time using in-product functionality, and may export available artifacts where the Service supports export.

**14.2 Export window.** Following termination or expiry, the Customer may export available Customer Data for **30 days**, provided the account is not suspended for breach or non-payment. Yulona has no obligation to retain Customer Data beyond that window.

**14.3 Deletion.** Yulona will delete Customer Personal Data from production systems within **30 days** of the later of: (a) expiry of the export window; or (b) the Customer's written instruction to delete.

**14.4 Credentials.** Connected Source credentials are deleted immediately on disconnection of the source or termination of the account, whichever is earlier.

**14.5 Backups and legal retention.** Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups until they expire on a rolling cycle, typically within **35 days**, during which they remain subject to this DPA and are not restored to production except for disaster recovery. Yulona may retain Personal Data where required by law, in which case it will retain it only for that purpose and will continue to protect it under this DPA.

**14.6 Certification.** Yulona will confirm deletion in writing on the Customer's written request.

**Special categories of Personal Data:** **not permitted.** The Customer is prohibited from introducing Restricted Data under clause 6.4 of the Terms of Use. If the Customer does so, it does so in breach and at its own risk.

**6. Data storage architecture (important).**

| Source type | What Yulona stores persistently |
|---|---|
| **Connected Sources** (e.g. PostgreSQL) | Connection metadata; access credentials (encrypted); **schema metadata only** — table and column names, data types, relationships, constraints, and statistical profiles. **No persistent replica of record-level contents.** |
| **Query Results** | Retrieved at query time and processed transiently. **However, results persist** where they appear in conversation history, saved charts, tables, dashboards, caches, and operational logs, until deleted. |
| **Uploaded Files** (CSV, Excel, JSON) | **Full file contents stored** for as long as the file remains in the Workspace. |
| **Prompts** | Stored as part of Workspace conversation history. |

**7. Categories of Data Subjects.** Determined by the Customer. May include the Customer's employees, contractors, customers, prospects, suppliers, partners, and other individuals whose records appear in the Customer's connected sources or uploaded files, together with the Customer's own Workspace members.

**8. Competent Supervisory Authority (for SCC purposes).** Determined in accordance with Clause 13 of the SCCs, being the Supervisory Authority of the Member State in which the Customer (as data exporter) is established, or in which its Article 27 representative is established, or the authority of the Member State in which the relevant Data Subjects are located.

# ANNEX II — Technical and organisational measures

> Review this Annex against what you actually implement before publishing. Overstating measures here is a contractual representation, not marketing copy.

**1. Encryption**
- TLS 1.2 or above for all data in transit, including connections to Customer data sources where supported by the source.
- Encryption at rest for stored files, databases, and backups.
- Data source credentials encrypted at rest using a managed key management service, with keys rotated on a defined schedule.

**2. Access control**
- Role-based access control within the Service, with Workspace-level isolation enforced at the application and data layer.
- Least-privilege internal access; production access limited to named personnel with a business need.
- Multi-factor authentication required for all administrative and production access.
- Access reviewed periodically and revoked promptly on role change or departure.

**3. Tenant isolation**
- Logical separation of Customer Workspaces; queries and artifacts scoped to a single Workspace.
- Authorisation checks enforced server-side on every request.

**4. Data minimisation**
- Schema-only persistence for Connected Sources.
- Customer-controlled selection of exposed schemas and tables.
- Read-only credential requirement imposed on Customers.

**5. Logging and monitoring**
- Audit logging of authentication, authorisation, configuration changes, and data source access.
- Centralised log collection with restricted access and defined retention.
- Alerting on anomalous access patterns and error conditions.

**6. Secure development**
- Version control, peer code review, and separation of development, staging, and production environments.
- Automated dependency vulnerability scanning and timely patching.
- Secrets managed outside source control.

**7. Resilience and continuity**
- Automated backups with defined retention and periodic restoration testing.
- Infrastructure redundancy in accordance with the hosting provider's regional architecture.

**8. Incident management**
- Documented incident response procedure with defined roles, severity classification, and escalation.
- Breach notification process aligned with clause 10 of this DPA.

**9. Personnel**
- Written confidentiality obligations for all personnel with access to Customer Personal Data.
- Security and data protection awareness training.
- Background screening where permitted by applicable law.

**10. Supplier management**
- Security and data protection due diligence before engaging a Subprocessor.
- Written data protection terms with all Subprocessors.
- Periodic review of Subprocessor security posture.

**11. Deletion**
- Deletion functionality available to Customers in-product.
- Defined deletion and backup expiry schedule as set out in clause 14.
