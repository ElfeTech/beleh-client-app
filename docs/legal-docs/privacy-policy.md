## 5. How the Service handles your connected business data

This section is important to understanding what actually leaves your systems.

**5.1 Connected Sources (databases).** When you connect a database, we store **connection metadata, encrypted credentials, and Schema Metadata only**. We do **not** create or maintain a persistent copy of the record-level contents of your database.

**5.2 Query-time processing.** When you submit a Prompt, the Service connects to your database, executes queries, and retrieves results. Those results are processed in memory and transmitted to our AI model provider to generate the response.

**5.3 What persists.** Although we do not replicate your database, **query results and derived content do persist** where they appear in: conversation history; saved charts, tables, and dashboards; performance caches; and operational and diagnostic logs. Assume that any data returned by a query and shown to you is retained in your Workspace until you delete it.

**5.4 Uploaded Files.** File contents are stored in full for as long as the file remains in your Workspace.

**5.5 AI model providers.** Prompts, Schema Metadata, and Query Results are transmitted to **Deepseek, Anthropic, Google** acting as our subprocessor(s) in order to generate responses. Under our agreements with those providers, Customer Data is **not** used to train their models. Processing takes place in **US-Central1**.

**5.6 No model training on your data.** We do not use Customer Data to train, fine-tune, or improve AI models for other customers or third parties. We use aggregated, de-identified usage statistics (for example, query volumes and latency) that do not identify you or reveal your data contents.
