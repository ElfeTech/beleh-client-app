## 2. What cookies and similar technologies are

**Cookies** are small text files placed on your device by a website. **Similar technologies** include:

- **Local storage** and **session storage** — browser-based storage used to hold preferences and interface state;
- **IndexedDB** — structured browser storage used for caching;
- **Pixels and tags** — small pieces of code that record that a page or email was opened;
- **Software development kits (SDKs)** — code embedded in applications that performs similar functions.

Throughout this Policy, we use "cookies" to refer to all of these.

Cookies may be **first-party** (set by us) or **third-party** (set by a provider we use). They may be **session** cookies (deleted when you close the browser) or **persistent** (retained until they expire or you delete them).

## 3. Why we use them

We use cookies to:

- keep you signed in and maintain your session securely;
- protect against cross-site request forgery and other attacks;
- remember which Workspace you are working in;
- balance load across our infrastructure;
- remember your preferences, such as theme and interface layout;
- record your cookie consent choices; and
- with your consent, understand how the product is used so we can improve it.

## 4. Cookies we use

### 4.1 Strictly necessary — always active

These are required for the Service to function and cannot be switched off. Blocking them will prevent sign-in and core features from working. Under most privacy laws, these do not require consent.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `firebaseLocalStorageDb` | Firebase Authentication | Maintains your authenticated session | IndexedDB | Until sign-out |
| `firebase_auth_token` | Firebase Authentication | Stores the authentication token issued at sign-in | Local storage | Until sign-out |
| `firebase_user`, `backend_user` | Yulona (first party) | Stores a snapshot of the signed-in user so the session can be restored | Local storage | Until sign-out |
| `activeWorkspaceId`, `activeSessionId` | Yulona (first party) | Remembers your selected Workspace and chat | Local storage | Until sign-out |

| `beleh.cookie-consent.v1` | Yulona (first party) | Records your cookie preferences | Local storage | 12 months |

These remember your choices. They are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us for analytics purposes.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ai-bi-theme-preference` | Yulona | Light / dark / system theme | Local storage | Until cleared |
| `beleh:ui:u:{uid}:sidebarCollapsed` | Yulona | Collapsed or expanded navigation state | Local storage | Until cleared |
| `beleh:ui:u:{uid}:ws:{workspaceId}:workspacesListSearch`, `workspaceDatasetSearch`, `workspaceSessionSearch`, `datasetsView` | Yulona | Remembers recent list filters and searches | Local storage | Until cleared |

### 4.3 Analytics — optional, consent required

These help us understand usage. **They load only after you opt in** through the cookie banner.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Note on session replay.** Microsoft Clarity may record interactions with pages. We configure it to mask text input and sensitive fields. It is not enabled inside authenticated analytics views where Customer Data is displayed. If we change this, we will update this Policy and seek fresh consent where required.

## 7. Your choices

**7.1 Cookie banner.** On your first visit you will see a banner offering **Accept all**, **Reject non-essential**, and **Customise**. Non-essential cookies are not set until you accept them. Rejecting non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use the Service.

**7.2 Changing your mind.** You can review or change your preferences at any time by selecting **"Cookie settings"** in the footer of any page. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and takes effect immediately for future processing.

**7.3 Consent records and re-prompting.** We store a record of your consent choice, including the date and the version of this Policy in force at the time. We will ask again after **12 months**, or sooner if we add a new category of cookie or a new provider.

**7.4 Browser controls.** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent sign-in and core features from working.

- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
- Safari: Settings → Privacy
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
- Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

**7.5 Opt-out tools.** You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites using the browser add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

**7.6 Global Privacy Control.** Where required by applicable law, we honour recognised opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) as a valid withdrawal of consent for non-essential cookies.

**7.7 Do Not Track.** Browser "Do Not Track" signals are not standardised and we do not respond to them. Use the controls in clauses 7.1–7.6 instead.
